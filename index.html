Ingress Protection Code

每一個 IP 數字背後，都是一台測試設備Behind every IP digit is a piece of test equipment

IP67 的「6」是外殼在滑石粉艙裡抽負壓 2～8 小時、目標抽過 80 倍外殼容積的空氣，「7」是把外殼壓到水面下 1 公尺泡 30 分鐘。在銘牌上點選數字，看它代表什麼；往下捲動，每一級測試都有動畫與原文參數。The “6” in IP67 means the enclosure spent 2–8 hours under vacuum in a talcum dust chamber, aiming to draw 80 enclosure volumes of air through it. The “7” means it sat 1 m under water for 30 minutes. Pick digits on the rating plate to see what they mean, then scroll down for an animation and the exact test parameters of every level.

First characteristic numeral · Clause 12、13

第一位數：探棒與粉塵艙First numeral: access probes and the dust chamber

第一位數同時考兩件事：人的身體部位（手背、手指、工具、鐵絲）碰不碰得到危險部位，以及固體異物進不進得去。1～4 級用探棒推，5、6 級進粉塵艙。拖動滑桿改開口尺寸，看探棒過不過得去。The first numeral covers two things at once: whether a body part (back of hand, finger, tool, wire) can reach hazardous parts, and whether solid foreign objects can get in. Levels 1–4 use probes; levels 5 and 6 use the dust chamber. Drag the sliders to change the opening size and see whether the probe gets through.

Second characteristic numeral · Clause 14

第二位數：從滴水到 80 °C 高壓水柱Second numeral: from dripping water to 80 °C high-pressure jets

九個等級用到六種設備：滴水箱、擺動管、手持噴嘴、噴水嘴、浸水槽、扇形噴嘴。動畫把實際測試時間壓縮播放，下方進度條顯示對應的實際測試時間。Nine levels use six kinds of apparatus: drip box, oscillating tube, hand-held spray nozzle, hose nozzle, immersion tank and fan-jet nozzle. Clause 6 · Dual coding

IP67 不等於能沖水：雙重標示IP67 does not mean jet-proof: dual coding

IPX1～IPX6 往下包含（過 IPX6 就算過 IPX1～5），但 IPX7、IPX8、IPX9 不包含噴水。浸水過關的外殼，標準直接視為「不適合承受 IPX5／IPX6 水柱」，除非另外測過並雙重標示。勾選你的產品通過了哪些測試：IPX1–IPX6 are cumulative (passing IPX6 covers IPX1–5), but IPX7, IPX8 and IPX9 do not cover water jets. An enclosure that only passed immersion is considered unsuitable for IPX5/IPX6 jets unless it was also tested and dual-coded. Tick the tests your product passed:

規則：噴水類取最高等級（6 已涵蓋 5），浸水類取最高等級，再用斜線串起來。標準把結果分成「通用（Versatile）」與「限定（Restricted）」兩種用途。Rule: take the highest jet level (6 covers 5) and the highest immersion level, then join them with a slash. The standard classes the result as “Versatile” or “Restricted” application.
